VISTACAP

Visual Intelligence Smart Cap for Accessibility, Translation & Perception

🔍 What is VISTACAP?

VISTACAP is a wearable AI-powered cap that helps users interact with their surroundings through real-time visual detection, sign language recognition, and live translation.

🧠 Key Features

  • 👁️ Object & terrain detection
  • 🧏 ISL/ASL sign language recognition
  • 🌐 Live translation of text and speech
  • 🔊 Audio alerts and guidance
  • 🖼️ Scene understanding and emotion detection

🔧 Hardware

  • 🧠 Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W
  • 🎥 Camera module
  • 🔊 Standard speaker
  • 🖥️ OLED/TFT display
  • 🔋 Battery pack

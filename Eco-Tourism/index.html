Chilika Lake

Asia's Largest Brackish Water Lagoon

Chilika Lake is a magnificent ecosystem spanning over 1,100 square kilometers across Odisha. This UNESCO-recognized wetland is home to over 160 species of birds, including the famous migratory flamingos that visit during winter. The lake supports diverse marine life, fishing communities, and offers breathtaking sunrise and sunset views.

🐦 160+ bird species including flamingos, pelicans, and herons
🐟 Rich marine biodiversity with prawns, crabs, and fish
🏝️ Multiple islands including Kalijai and Honeymoon Island
🌊

Satabhaya

The Disappearing Village - A Climate Change Story

Satabhaya is a poignant example of climate change impact on coastal communities. This village on the shores of Chilika Lake has been gradually submerging due to rising sea levels and coastal erosion. Once home to over 1,200 families, many residents have been forced to relocate as their ancestral homes disappear beneath the waters. It serves as a powerful reminder of environmental challenges facing coastal India.

🏘️ Historic fishing village affected by sea level rise
🌊 Witness to ongoing coastal erosion and climate impact
📚 Important case study for environmental awareness
